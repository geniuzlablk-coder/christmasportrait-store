Questions

Before you order

How fast will I get my portraits?

Digital portraits are delivered by email within 24 hours of your photo upload. Printed gifts (mugs, canvas, hoodies, stockings) are produced per order — order by Dec 20 to guarantee Christmas delivery.

What kind of photo works best?

A clear photo where faces are visible and reasonably well lit. Phone photos are perfect. Group shots up to 4 people fit the Family Pack; pets can join any portrait — just mention them in the order notes.

What if I don't love my portrait?

That's what the Love-It Guarantee is for: tell us within 14 days and we re-make your portraits in a different style free, or refund you in full. No forms, no interrogation.

Can I print the portraits myself?

Yes — files are print-ready at 300 DPI, sized for Christmas cards, canvas, and framed prints. Or skip the printer run and add the mug, canvas, or stocking from the gift shop and we handle production.

What is the Dec 20 cutoff?

December 20, 2026 is the last day to order printed gifts with guaranteed Christmas delivery. Digital portraits and downloads stay instant right up to Dec 25 — the ultimate last-minute save.

Do you ship internationally?

Digital portraits and downloads go anywhere instantly. Printed gifts are produced at facilities close to the recipient in 32 countries, which keeps international delivery fast and affordable.
