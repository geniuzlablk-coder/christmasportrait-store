Questions

Before you order

How fast will I get my portraits?

Digital portraits are delivered by email within 24 hours of your photo upload. Printed gifts (mugs, canvas, sweaters) are produced per order by our print partners — order by the cutoff date shown on each product to guarantee Christmas delivery.

What kind of photo works best?

A clear photo where faces are visible and reasonably well lit. Phone photos are perfect. Group shots up to 4 people fit the Family Pack; pets can join any portrait — just mention them in the order notes.

What if I don't love my portrait?

That's what the Love-It Guarantee is for: tell us within 14 days and we re-make your portraits in a different style free, or refund you in full. No forms, no interrogation.

Can I print the portraits myself?

Yes — files are print-ready at 300 DPI, sized for Christmas cards, canvas, and framed prints. Or skip the printer run and add the mug, canvas, or puzzle from the gift shop and we handle production.

What is the Dec 20 cutoff?

December 20, 2026 is the last day to order portraits with guaranteed Christmas delivery — and the last day the free Santa Letter comes with every portrait. Digital portraits ordered after Dec 20 still arrive in 24h, but we can't promise they'll beat Santa.

How does the GluteBoss gift work?

The Christmas Strong Gift gives GluteBoss Annual ($99.99) plus a free Single portrait ($39 value) for $99.99 total. The portrait is delivered within 24h; the GluteBoss program starts January 5. It's giftable — add the recipient's email at checkout.

Do you ship internationally?

Digital portraits go anywhere instantly. Printed gifts are produced close to the recipient through Gelato's local printers in 32 countries, which keeps international delivery fast and affordable.
